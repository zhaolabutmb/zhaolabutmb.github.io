We started an informal computational seminar series on campus. The initial participating groups include our lab and five other UTMB computational groups — Pettitt, Werner, Sadygov, Golovko and Khanipov. The topics focus on ML/AI-driven modeling works in computational biology, computational chemistry and biophysics. 

It is every other Thursday noon at RB6, 5.406. The first speaker is Dr. Zhiyuan Song from our group. More schedules and details are available [here][1].
