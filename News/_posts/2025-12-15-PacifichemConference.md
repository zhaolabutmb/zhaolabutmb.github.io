Dr. Zhao gave an invited talk at [The 2025 International Chemical Congress of Pacific Basin Societies (Pacifichem)][1]. Pacifichem is considered as the world's largest chemistry conference, organized by national chemistry societies of seven Pacific countries. It helds every five years in Honolulu, Hawaii.
