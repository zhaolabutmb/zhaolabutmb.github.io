Dr. Zhao gave an invited talk at [the 2025 International Chemical Congress of Pacific Basin Societies (Pacifichem)][1]. Pacifichem is considered as the world's largest chemistry conference, organized by national chemistry societies of seven Pacific countries. It is held every five years in Honolulu, Hawaii. 

Dr. Zhongpei Fang from Dr. Minkui Luo's lab presented a poster about our collaborative project on tRNA synthetases.
