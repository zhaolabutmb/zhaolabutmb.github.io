Excited to announce the launch of LiverHomo, our interactive human liver single-cell atlas and protein interaction database, integrating over 1.15 million cells across healthy and diseased liver conditions. The platform provides interactive exploration of gene expression, protein–protein interactions, and cell–cell communication networks, along with an AI-powered assistant for natural-language queries. Explore <a href="https://www.liverhomo.org">www.LiverHomo.org</a>. Read the preprint <a href="/papers/LiverHomo/">here</a>.
