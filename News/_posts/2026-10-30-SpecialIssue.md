Dr. Zhao is co-editing a special issue themed on **[Integrative AI for Multi-Scale Modeling of Host–Pathogen and Microbiome Interactions](https://www.frontiersin.org/research-topics/85752/integrative-ai-for-multi-scale-modeling-of-host-pathogen-and-microbiome-interactions)**. This cross-journal collection spans *Frontiers in Cellular and Infection Microbiology*, *Frontiers in Bioinformatics*, and *Frontiers in Systems Biology*. We invite contributions bridging molecular modeling, cellular signaling, and infection outcomes through integrative ML/AI developments before the **May 30, 2027** deadline.
