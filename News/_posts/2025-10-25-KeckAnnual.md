The lab attended the 35th Keck Annual Conference, held at Rice University. This year’s anniversary honored Dr. Pettitt and Dr. Kathy Matthews for their outstanding contributions to establishing the Keck Center, a training partnership connecting TMC universities across the Houston–Galveston area. It was inspiring to learn that, since its founding in 1990, the Keck Center has maintained a strong focus on computational research. Thank you, Monte and Kathy, for your lasting impact!
