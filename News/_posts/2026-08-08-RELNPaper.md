We are excited to contribute to an international collaboration investigating how rare genetic variants may shape resilience to autosomal dominant Alzheimer’s disease (AD). Our group contributed protein–protein interaction modeling to examine the effects of the protective RELN-COLBOS variant and computational pathogenicity predictions of additional genetic variants. Congratulations to the entire international team and our lab members Zhiyuan Song and Rui Tang! Read more <a href="/papers/RELN/">here</a>.
