Our E. coli paper was accepted on Nature Communications. In this study, we developed an integrative framework that combines structural modeling (PrePPI), protein language models (Topsy-Turvy), and evolutionary information (ZEPPI) to predict protein-protein interactions at the proteome scale. Applying this framework to the E. coli proteome, we identified hundreds of functionally coherent subnetworks — without explicit functional annotations, the model reveals meaningful biological predictions and enables functional annotation of previously uncharacterized proteins.
