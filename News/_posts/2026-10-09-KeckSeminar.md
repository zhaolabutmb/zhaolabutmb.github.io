Dr. Zhao was invited by a trainee committee to present at the [Keck Seminar Series](https://gulfcoastconsortia.org/home/training/keck-seminar/), organized by Gulf Coast Consortia and hosted at Rice University. Dr. Zhao gave a talk titled “Proteins in Context: Using AI to Connect Molecular Interactions to Disease”. The keck seminar serves GCC-supported training programs across eight regional institutions.
