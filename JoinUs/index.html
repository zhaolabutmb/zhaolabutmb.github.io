Join Us

We work at the intersection of AI, structural biology, biophysics, and multi-omics. Our lab develops computational/AI approaches to study protein interactions, molecular systems, and disease mechanisms, with applications in target/drug discovery and translational biology. In general, we look for candidates with such backgrounds: (1) major in biology with rich experience in bioinformatics (students); or (2) major in quantitative science with experience developing ML/AI models for biological systems (students/postdocs/technicians).

Open Positions

  • Postdoctoral Fellow — Computational biology / AI for protein interactions, systems biology, or structure-based modeling
  • Research Associate — Support computational biology projects, data analysis, and workflow development
  • PhD Student — Research in protein interaction prediction, multi-omics integration, or protein design

We welcome applicants with backgrounds in computational biology, bioinformatics, chemistry, physics, machine learning, or related quantitative fields. If you are interested, please email Dr. Zhao with your CV and a brief statement of interest.
